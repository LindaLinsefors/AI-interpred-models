1"""Every block's write, from what a reader of these stories knows at each word.
2
3The text is a synthetic story language: rows of packed stories, each from one of 16 "worlds"
4(its own pool of names, places and objects), told in ~25 sentence templates: an introduction
5("once there was a boy named N and a girl named N ."), placements ("N is in the L .", "there
6is a O in the L ."), events (goes to / takes / drops / gives / looks at ("it is A and A .") /
7sees K things / finds and loses K coins ("now N has K coins .") / intransitive verb with an
8adverb / other transitive verbs), questions with their answers ("where is N ? she is in the
9L .", "where is the O ? it is in the L .", "who has the O ? N has it .", "how many coins does
10N have ? she has K coins ."), and dialogue ("N says to N , \" i am A / we are in the L / the O
11is in the L / i have the O . \"", "N answers , ...").
12
13The model computes, and this program reproduces, one shared reading of the story:
14 1. Story (below) keeps the world state symbolically: the cast and their genders, where each
15 person and object is, who holds what, how many coins each person has, the last list of
16 things seen, which kinds of events the story and each person have used, the question
17 being answered, the speaker and the addressee, and who "he"/"she" refer to.
18 2. World infers which of the 16 worlds the story is set in from the distinct words seen.
19 3. Featurizer names the concepts at each word (the CONCEPT FIELDS listed below): the word
20 and its two predecessors, its grammatical slot, what the slot expects next (an open
21 choice, where the world's prior applies, or a closed one among the story's own people,
22 places and objects), the word the
23 world state fixes next (a question's answer, the next character to be placed, the coin
24 count, the held object after "the girl with the"), the words it allows (objects at the
25 subject's place after "takes the", people at the same place after "says to", ...), the
26 world, the subject's role and preconditions, and the story's characters, places, objects.
27 4. Every concept value has one fitted signature (SIGNATURES, 384 numbers; the previous
28 words and a few shortcut fields get theirs from smaller tables through a projection).
29 The weighted sum of the active signatures is the reader's state at this word.
30 5. A mixing layer (MIX_IN/MIX_OUT, 1024 units) adds to the state what a plain sum cannot
31 express: effects of combinations of concepts. It is a fitted approximation, not a
32 recovered circuit. Its strongest units, read off on held-out rows: at the verb slot,
33 units that combine the subject's role (kinds of events it has done) with its
34 preconditions and promote that role's verbs (finders: finds/plays/shivers/cheers;
35 after seeing things: likes/helps/kicks; nothing possible: is/goes); in the
36 introduction, units that cross the world with the gender ("a boy named" in worlds 4,
37 13, 14 -> jack tom evan noah; "a girl named" -> lucy zoe ava emma); and many units at
38 "N is in" that write which character is being placed (the next word there is always
39 "the", so this is context the blocks carry forward, not a prediction).
40 6. READOUT turns the state into all twelve blocks' writes (OFFSET + READOUT . state).
41 Everything numeric was fitted on 20k training rows so that each block's write matches
42 the model's and the sum of the twelve writes gives the model's next-word prediction
43 (most of what the blocks write is that prediction and the context behind it).
44
45Long tables: CLASSES (word -> word class), GENDER (name -> boy/girl), WORLD_* (how strongly
46each word points to each world), CONCEPTS (the named concept values), SIGNATURES, MIX_* and
47WRITE_MAP (fitted numbers, 5-8 bit with scales), OFFSET (each block's mean write).

49CONCEPT FIELDS (Featurizer.step returns them):
50d holds categorical values (strings) and multi-valued ones (lists, or (value, weight) pairs):
51 w0, w1, w2 current / previous / two-back word ('' at row start)
52 c0 class of current word (NAME/OBJ/LOC/ADJ/NUM/PLUR/VI/VT/ADV/ORD/PRON or the word)
53 slot grammar state after w0 (story3.Story.slot), e.g. '|S goes to the',
54 'A.whereN|S is in the', 'END |S takes the O .', 'Q|we are in the', 'open'
55 slot3 backoff: ctx + last 3 abstracted items
56 ans the next word the world state fixes (list of 0 or 1-2 words)
57 cand candidate next words the world state allows (a set; may be empty)
58 evt kinds of event sentences told so far in this story (multi-valued)
59 world posterior over the 16 story worlds, as (world, probability) pairs
60 wx the same, crossed with `expect` (the kind of word the slot expects next)
61 expect START/NAME/LOC/OBJ/ADJ/VERB/ADV/OTHER; NAMEc/LOCc/OBJc when the word must be one
62 the story already holds
63 addr at a verb slot: whether the subject was just spoken to (yes@VERB/no@VERB); at a
64 sentence start: who was last spoken to
65 list how many sentences ago someone saw a list of things (0-3, 4 = longer), with the
66 expected word kind ('the first one' and the listed things stay likely for a while)
67 clast at a verb slot: the kind of the subject's own latest event (people tend to repeat
68 or follow on: goes->goes, finds->finds, sees->takes, drops->takes)
69 recent asked where an object is: the place read most recently (another shortcut)
70 nc how many candidates the world state allows, with the expected word kind
71 prov how a question's answer is known (object held / free / never seen), '/part' when
72 the story began before the row did
73 count while placing objects: how many are placed (at most 3); in the introduction: how
74 many characters are named (at most 4)

78 others at a sentence start: the other characters
79 naive where the asked-about object was first seen (the model half-believes it is still there)
80 evx, prex evt and pre crossed with expect, at sentence starts and verb slots only

123class World:
124 """Which of the 16 story worlds we are in. Each world has its own cast, places and objects
125 (its vocabulary); `affinity[word]` holds log P(word appears in a story | world) for the
126 words that tell worlds apart. The posterior sums the affinities of the distinct words seen
127 since the story began."""

129 def __init__(self, words, affinity, prior):
130 import numpy as np
131 self.np = np
132 self.affinity = {w: affinity[:, j].astype(np.float64) / 8 for j, w in enumerate(words)}
133 self.prior = prior.astype(np.float64) / 8
134 self.reset()

136 def reset(self):
137 self.score = self.prior.copy()
138 self.seen = set()
139 self._post = None

141 def feed(self, w):

143 if a is not None and w not in self.seen:
144 self.seen.add(w)

149 if self._post is None:
150 e = self.np.exp(self.score - self.score.max())

674def expect(slot):
675 """The kind of word the grammar expects next at this slot; a trailing 'c' marks a closed
676 choice (the word must be one the story already holds: a person, place or object in it)."""
677 head, _, body = slot.partition('|')
678 items = body.split()
679 last = items[-1] if items else ''
680 prev = items[-2] if len(items) > 1 else ''
681 if slot in ('EOS', 'close') or head.startswith('END') or head.startswith('Qend'):
682 return 'START'
683 if head.startswith('A.'):
684 return 'OTHER' # an answer sentence: its words are fixed
685 if (last == 'to' and prev == 'says') or (last == 'to' and 'gives' in items):
686 return 'NAMEc'
687 if last == 'named':
688 return 'NAME'
689 if 'named' in items or items == ['the']:
690 return 'OTHER' # 'a boy/girl', 'the boy/girl'
691 if last == 'the' and prev == 'in' and (items[:2] == ['there', 'is'] or head == 'Q'):
692 return 'LOCc'
693 if last == 'the' and prev in ('in', 'to'):
694 return 'LOC'
695 if last == 'the':
696 return 'OBJc'
697 if last == 'a' and ('is' in items or ':' in items or ',' in items or 'and' in items):
698 return 'OBJ'
699 if last in ('is', 'and', 'am') and (items[:1] == ['it'] or 'am' in items):
700 return 'ADJ'
701 if last.startswith('S'):
702 return 'VERB'
703 if last == 'V':
704 return 'ADV'
705 return 'OTHER'

119 words: list[str]
120 values: list[tuple[float, object]] # the options the words fit, with their posteriors
121
122 def value(self, at: int | None = None) -> object:
123 """The value the words said (or its `at`-th part), which they must leave in no doubt."""
124 values = {v if at is None else v[at] for _, v in self.values} # type: ignore[index]
125 assert len(values) == 1, values
126 return values.pop()
127
128 def posterior(self, at: int) -> dict[object, float]:
129 out: dict[object, float] = {}
130 for p, v in self.values:
131 out[v[at]] = out.get(v[at], 0.0) + p # type: ignore[index]
132 return out

139def choose(options: Iterable[Option]) -> Choice:
140 """One of `options`, said word by word: each word is drawn from the options whose words so
141 far fit, by weight. Returns the words and the options they fit."""
142 live = [(o.weight, o) for o in options if o.weight > 0]
143 assert live, "nothing to choose from"
144 words: list[str] = []
145 for i in itertools.count():
146 if any(len(o.words) == i for _, o in live):
147 assert all(len(o.words) == i for _, o in live), "an option's words begin another's"
148 total = sum(p for p, _ in live)
149 return Said(words, [(p / total, o.value) for p, o in live])
150 total = sum(p for p, _ in live)
151 word = yield [(p / total, o.words[i]) for p, o in live]
152 live = [(p * o.words[i].prob(word), o) for p, o in live if word in o.words[i]]
153 assert live, f"{word!r} is impossible here"
154 words.append(word)
155 raise AssertionError("unreachable")

162class Latent:
163 """A value the text never states. Sampling knows it; replay starts from the uniform prior,
164 and each choice whose options depend on it updates the belief by Bayes' rule."""
165
166 def __init__(self, n: int, value: int | None, frozen: bool = False):
167 self.belief = np.full(n, 1 / n) if value is None else np.eye(n)[value]
168 self.frozen = frozen # a replay ignoring it keeps the prior (`ablate`)
169
170 def support(self) -> list[tuple[int, float]]:
171 return [(k, float(b)) for k, b in enumerate(self.belief) if b > 0]
172
173 def update(self, said: Said, at: int) -> None:
174 """Condition on the words of a choice whose values hold this latent's value at `at`."""
175 if not self.frozen:
176 posterior = np.zeros_like(self.belief)
177 for k, p in said.posterior(at).items():
178 posterior[k] = p # type: ignore[index]
179 self.belief = posterior / posterior.sum()

321@dataclass
322class State:
323 """What the generator tracks. Labels read it as each word is chosen."""
324
325 config: Config
326 rng: random.Random | None # draws the latent values while sampling; None while replaying
327 ignore: frozenset[str] = frozenset() # families a replay pretends not to know (`ablate`)
328 theme: Latent = field(init=False)
329 cast: list[Character] = field(default_factory=list)
330 things: list[Thing] = field(default_factory=list)
331 places: list[str] = field(default_factory=list)
332 last: dict[str, Character] = field(default_factory=dict) # the latest mention of each gender
333 countable: str | None = None
334 setting: bool = True # still telling what lies where
335 actor: Character | None = None # the last sentence's
336 addressed: Character | None = None # whom the last sentence spoke to
337 counted: Character | None = None # whose coins the last sentence changed
338 listed: list[Thing] = field(default_factory=list) # what the last sentence listed
339 sentences: int = 0
340 slot: str = "start" # label: the kind of choice being made
341 families: frozenset[str] = frozenset() # label: the hidden families it depends on
342
343 def __post_init__(self) -> None:
344 self.theme = self.latent(N_THEMES, "theme")
345
346 def latent(self, n: int, family: str) -> Latent:
347 if not getattr(self.config, family):
348 return Latent(1, 0)
349 return Latent(n, None if self.rng is None else self.rng.randrange(n), frozen=family in self.ignore)
350
351 def at(self, slot: str, *families: str) -> None:
352 self.slot, self.families = slot, frozenset(families)
353
354 def themed(self, cls: str, theme: int) -> Words:
355 return themed(cls, theme if self.config.theme else None)
356
357 def kind(self, thing: Thing) -> int | None:
358 return KIND[thing.noun] if self.config.kinds and "kinds" not in self.ignore else None
359
360 def mention(self, c: Character) -> None:
361 self.last[c.gender] = c
362
363 def held(self, c: Character) -> list[Thing]:
364 return [t for t in self.things if t.holder is c]
365
366 def visible(self, c: Character) -> list[Thing]:
367 return [t for t in self.things if t.where() == c.place]
368
369 def others_here(self, c: Character) -> list[Character]:
370 return [o for o in self.cast if o is not c and o.place == c.place]
371
372 def unused(self, words: Words) -> Words:
373 return words.without(t.noun for t in self.things).without(self.places)

376def stream(config: Config = FULL, rng: random.Random | None = None, states: list[State] | None = None,
377 ignore: frozenset[str] = frozenset()) -> Program:
378 """Stories one after another, each ended by [EOS]: the text rows are cut from. Sampling
379 passes `rng`, which draws the latent values; each story's state is appended to `states`."""
380 while True:
381 s = State(config, rng, ignore)
382 if states is not None:
383 states.append(s)
384 yield from story(s)

387def story(s: State) -> Program:
388 s.at("setup")
389 yield from say("once there was")
390 yield from introduce(s)
391 for c in s.cast:
392 s.at("setup")
393 yield from say(f"{c.name} is in the")
394 s.at("place", "theme")
395 c.place = yield from place(s, c, new=1.5 if s.places else 1.0)
396 s.at("setup")
397 yield from say(".")
398 s.at("thing", "theme")
399 yield from thing_there(s, (yield from choose(thing_options(s, 1.0))))
400 while True:
401 s.at("sentence")
402 said = yield from choose(sentence_options(s))
403 match said.value(0):
404 case "end":
405 return
406 case "thing":
407 yield from thing_there(s, said)
408 continue
409 case "now":
410 yield from say(".")
411 s.actor = s.addressed = s.counted = None
412 s.listed = []
413 case "question":
414 yield from answer(s, said.value(1))
415 s.actor = s.addressed = s.counted = None
416 s.listed = []
417 case "actor":
418 actor = said.value(1)
419 s.mention(actor) # type: ignore[arg-type]
420 yield from event(s, actor) # type: ignore[arg-type]
421 s.setting = False
422 s.sentences += 1

425def introduce(s: State) -> Program:
426 """'a girl named mia and a boy named leo .': two to four characters."""
427 while True:
428 s.at("setup")
429 gender = (yield from choose([option(0.5, ["a girl named"], "girl"), option(0.5, ["a boy named"], "boy")])).value()
430 s.at("name", "theme")
431 said = yield from choose(option(b, [s.themed(gender, z).without(c.name for c in s.cast)], (None, z)) # type: ignore[arg-type]
432 for z, b in s.theme.support())
433 s.theme.update(said, at=1)
434 name = said.words[0]
435 c = Character(name, gender, s.latent(N_TEMPERAMENTS, "temperament")) # type: ignore[arg-type]
436 s.cast.append(c)
437 s.mention(c)
438 more = {1: 1.0, 2: 0.5, 3: 0.3}.get(len(s.cast), 0.0)
439 s.at("setup")
440 if (yield from choose([option(more, ["and"], True), option(1 - more, ["."], False)])).value() is False:
441 return

444def place(s: State, c: Character, new: float) -> Generator[Dist, str, str]:
445 """A place `c` is not at: one of the story's, or (by weight `new`, while it has fewer than
446 four) a new one, favouring the theme's."""
447 known = [p for p in s.places if p != c.place]
448 new = new if len(s.places) < 4 else 0.0
449 said = yield from choose([
450 *(option(1.0, [p], ("known", None)) for p in known),
451 *(option(b * new, [s.unused(s.themed("place", z))], ("new", z)) for z, b in s.theme.support()),
452 ])
453 if said.value(0) == "new":
454 s.theme.update(said, at=1)
455 s.places.append(said.words[0])
456 return said.words[0]

465 """'... in the barn .': where the thing `said` lies."""
466 s.theme.update(said, at=1)
467 noun = said.words[-1]

473def sentence_options(s: State) -> list[Option]:
474 """How the next sentence starts: the end of the story, another thing (while setting the
475 scene), 'now she has ...' after coins change, a question, or a character who acts."""
476 end = 0.0 if s.sentences < 8 else 1.0 if s.sentences >= 24 else 0.1
477 rest = 1 - end
478 options = [option(end, [EOS], ("end",))]
479 if s.setting and len(s.things) < 3:
480 options += thing_options(s, rest * 0.6)
481 rest *= 0.4
482 if s.counted is not None:
483 c = s.counted
484 has = ["has", NUMBER[c.coins] if c.coins else "no", s.countable]
485 refs = references(s, c, pronoun=0.6, name=0.4)
486 options += [option(rest * 0.5 * p, ["now", *ref, *has], ("now",)) for p, ref in refs]
487 rest *= 0.5
488 asks = questions(s)
489 if s.config.questions and s.sentences >= 2 and asks:
490 options += [option(rest * 0.15 * p, words, ("question", q)) for p, words, q in asks]
491 rest *= 0.85
492 weights = {c: 1.0 + (c is s.actor) + 2.0 * (c is s.addressed) for c in s.cast}
493 total = sum(weights.values())
494 for c, w in weights.items():
495 refs = references(s, c, pronoun=1.5, name=1.0, describe=0.6 if s.config.describe else 0.0)
496 options += [option(rest * w / total * p, ref, ("actor", c)) for p, ref in refs]
497 return options

500def references(s: State, c: Character, pronoun: float = 0.0, name: float = 0.0, describe: float = 0.0,
501 case: dict[str, str] = SUBJECT) -> list[tuple[float, list[str]]]:
502 """Ways to refer to `c`, with probabilities: by name; by pronoun, if `c` is the latest of
503 its gender mentioned; as 'the girl with the kite', if `c` holds something."""
504 held = s.held(c)
505 ways = [(name, [c.name])]
506 if s.last.get(c.gender) is c:
507 ways.append((pronoun, [case[c.gender]]))
508 ways += [(describe / len(held), ["the", c.gender, "with the", t.noun]) for t in held]
509 total = sum(w for w, _ in ways)
510 return [(w / total, words) for w, words in ways if w > 0]

538 """The true answer: 'it is in the barn .', 'leo has it .', 'nobody has it .', 'she is in

569def event_probs(s: State, c: Character, temperament: int) -> dict[str, float]:
570 """What `c` may do next, given its temperament: weights the temperament and the laws set,
571 normalised over what is possible; an answer, when `c` was just spoken to, half the time."""
572 held, visible, here = s.held(c), s.visible(c), s.others_here(c)
573 possible = {
574 "go": True,
575 "take": any(t.holder is None for t in visible),
576 "drop": bool(held),
577 "give": bool(held) and bool(here),
578 "look": bool(visible),
579 "use": bool(visible),
580 "find": c.coins <= 18,
581 "lose": c.coins >= 2,
582 "say": bool(here),
583 "see": len(s.things) <= 8,
584 "act": True,
585 }
586 weights = TEMPERAMENT_EVENT_WEIGHT[temperament] if s.config.temperament else BASE_EVENT_WEIGHT
587 w = {e: weights[e] for e, ok in possible.items() if ok and s.config.has(e)}
588 if s.config.laws and "laws" not in s.ignore: # the laws share going and acting's weight
589 holds = facts(s, c)
590 tilt = {"go": w["go"], "act": w["act"]}
591 for law in LAWS:
592 tilt[law.favoured(holds)] *= LAW_BOOST
593 pair, tilted = w["go"] + w["act"], tilt["go"] + tilt["act"]
594 w["go"], w["act"] = pair * tilt["go"] / tilted, pair * tilt["act"] / tilted
595 answering = 0.5 if s.config.dialogue and s.addressed is c else 0.0
596 total = sum(w.values())
597 return {"answer": answering} | {e: (1 - answering) * x / total for e, x in w.items()}

600def event(s: State, c: Character) -> Program:
601 """What `c` does, said by its verb; a transitive verb says which visible thing it may be
602 used on."""
603 s.at("event", "temperament", "laws", "kinds")
604 visible = s.visible(c)
605 options = []
606 for t, b in c.temperament.support():
607 for e, p in event_probs(s, c, t).items():
608 if e == "use":
609 options += [option(b * p / len(visible), [uses(s.kind(x))], (e, t, x)) for x in visible]
610 elif e == "act":
611 options.append(option(b * p, [ACTS[t] if s.config.temperament else PLAIN_ACTS], (e, t, None)))
612 else:
613 options.append(option(b * p, [VERB[e]], (e, t, None)))
614 said = yield from choose(options)
615 c.temperament.update(said, at=1)
616 e = said.value(0)
617 listed, s.listed = s.listed, []
618 speaker = s.actor
619 s.actor, s.addressed, s.counted = c, None, None
620 match e:
621 case "go":
622 yield from say("to the")
623 s.at("place", "theme")
624 c.place = yield from place(s, c, new=1.0)
625 s.at("end")
626 yield from say(".")
627 case "take":
628 s.at("object")
629 options = [option(0.4 if listed else 1.0, ["the", x.noun, "."], x) for x in visible if x.holder is None]
630 options += [option(0.6, ["the", lx.ORDINALS[i], "one ."], x) for i, x in enumerate(listed)
631 if x.holder is None and x.where() == c.place]
632 took = (yield from choose(options)).value()
633 took.holder = c # type: ignore[union-attr]
634 case "drop":
635 s.at("object")

677 yield from say("to")

711def adjective(s: State, kind: int | None, other_than: str = "") -> Generator[Dist, str, str]:

713 said = yield from choose(option(b, [adjectives(kind, z if s.config.theme else None).without([other_than])], (None, z))

719def utterance(s: State, speaker: Character, to: Character) -> Program:
720 """Something true the speaker says to `to`: 'i have the kite', 'you have the cup', 'the
721 ball is in the barn', 'we are in the barn', 'i am happy', 'i have five coins'."""
722 kinds: list[list[Option]] = [
723 [option(1.0, ["i have the", x.noun], ("have", None)) for x in s.held(speaker)],
724 [option(1.0, ["you have the", x.noun], ("have", None)) for x in s.held(to)],
725 [option(1.0, ["the", x.noun, "is in the", x.place], ("lies", None)) for x in s.things if x.holder is None],
726 [option(1.0, ["we are in the", speaker.place], ("here", None))],
727 [option(b, ["i am", adjectives(None, z if s.config.theme else None)], ("am", z)) for z, b in s.theme.support()],
728 ]
729 if s.countable:
730 kinds.append([option(1.0, ["i have", NUMBER[speaker.coins] if speaker.coins else "no", s.countable], ("have", None))])
731 kinds = [k for k in kinds if k]
732 said = yield from choose(
733 Option(o.weight / len(kinds) / (1 if o.value[0] == "am" else len(k)), o.words, o.value) # type: ignore[index]
734 for k in kinds for o in k
735 )
736 if said.value(0) == "am":
737 s.theme.update(said, at=1)

740def sample(program: Program, n_words: int, rng: random.Random) -> list[str]:
741 """The first `n_words` words `program` produces, drawn with `rng`."""
742 words: list[str] = []
743 dist = next(program)
744 while True:
745 u = rng.random()
746 for k, (p, part) in enumerate(dist): # parts may be one Words object several times
747 if u < p or k == len(dist) - 1:
748 break
749 u -= p
750 words.append(part.sample(rng))
751 if len(words) == n_words:
752 return words
753 dist = program.send(words[-1])
