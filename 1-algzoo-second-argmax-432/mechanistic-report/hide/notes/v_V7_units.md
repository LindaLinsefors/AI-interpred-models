# Verification bundle V7_units

## Summary

All three claims (O1, O2, O3) reproduce: O2 and O3 exactly (deterministic), O1 within 0.01 on fresh seeds and to the last digit on the lead's seed. The interpretation survives for units 0, 11 and 10 and fails or needs rewording in four places.

WHAT SURVIVES
- The zeros of units 0, 11, 10 belong to the age of the maximum, not to the latest record-like event. At the matched age they are zero for every age of the runner-up (unit 0: 0.98-1.00; unit 11: 0.95-0.99; unit 10: 0.88-0.99). A new runner-up without a unit-4 firing does not produce them at its own age (0.010 and 0.003 for units 0 and 11).
- They also occur when the maximum is the first positive input (unit 0: 1.000, unit 10: 0.949, unit 11: 0.877) and for x = (1, 0, ..., 0).
- They are causally needed when a record arrives. Forcing the unit to keep its previous-step value at the record step, then continuing the run, changes P(answer = old max) from 0.978 to 0.039 (unit 0, old max 2 steps old), 0.976 to 0.292 (unit 11, 5 steps) and 0.970 to 0.085 (unit 10, 6 steps). Other units in the same rows stay at 0.93 or above, except unit 12 at age 5 (0.353) and unit 11 at age 6 (0.692).

WHAT NEEDS CHANGING
1. Unit 12 is not cut to zero. In final states (t = 9) it is exactly zero in 13.9% of cases at age 4; its pre-activation there is positive in 87% (median +1.18 M). The forcing test barely moves the answer (0.981 to 0.812). Its trough matters one step earlier: forcing it at age 3 gives 0.983 to 0.171.
2. "Cut by the ReLU" is literally true only for unit 0 (pre-activation median -2.95 M). Units 11 and 10 only graze zero (median -0.46 M and -0.47 M; -0.02 M and -0.46 M for an isolated maximum).
3. "The one that is not voted against" holds only for maximum ages 1, 2, 5, 6 (the position with the smallest negative vote among 0..8 is the maximum's in 0.927, 0.997, 0.998, 0.981 of sequences). At ages 3, 4, 7, 8 that rate is 0.000: the maximum's position gets -29, -18, -41, -62 M of negative votes and wins through positive votes (+64, +53, +72, +97 M against +34 to +44 M elsewhere). At age 9 m ranks the maximum first in only 0.306 of sequences. Index 9 must be left out throughout.
4. The comparator swing is not proportional to M - S and does not shrink to nothing: 7-21% of it remains as S approaches M, which fits M - 0.87 S (the lead's notebook figure). The wave units' swing also scales with the gap (20-63% remains), so gap-scaling is a matter of degree between the two groups.
5. The sweep's threshold pattern (comparator 8 at S, the other three near M) is specific to a maximum 4 steps old. Over 240 configurations the comparator with the lowest threshold is 6, 1, 8, 8, 8, 7, 6, 6, 1 for maximum ages 1..9, and at ages 5-8 no threshold lies near S. All nine probe values are answered correctly in 77.9% of configurations.

All sampled numbers use fresh seeds 5001-5055, 2M sequences per experiment (400k per batch).
