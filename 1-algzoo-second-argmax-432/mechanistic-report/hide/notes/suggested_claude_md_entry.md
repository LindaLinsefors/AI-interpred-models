## Findings (mechanistic report, 2026-11-19; numbers independently reproduced unless marked)

- **Running answer.** $h_t(x_0..x_t) = h_9(0,\dots,0,x_0..x_t)$ exactly, so $s_t = U h_t$ can be read at any step (index $9 - a$ = input of age $a$): it names the runner-up of the prefix in 96–99% of states with $S_t > 0$. This is common among retrained networks (354 of 379 with accuracy ≥ 0.90).
- **Scores.** On steps below the runner-up, entries two or more steps old move one index per step. The newest entry is a comparison by value, $s[9] - s[\text{runner-up}] \approx 61.6\,(x_t - S)$ (a property of the weights); older entries are clipped. The resting-map shift matrix $\Sigma = U_o A U_o^+$ has $\Sigma[i, i+1] = 0.89$–$1.00$ for $i \le 6$; the resting gate pattern holds in only 10% of states.
- **Mode coordinates and update rule.** $\zeta_m = \ell_m^\top h[o]$ ($o$ = 13 non-pulse units). Exactly: $\zeta_m(t) = \lambda_m\zeta_m(t-1)$ + input term + pulse terms (units 4, 5, 9) + a term per comparator that the input switched off + clipped wave units. Coefficients for level, runner and clock are in report section 5 and in the lab notebook.
- **Two score vectors.** $s = m + V$, $V$ = the readout through comparators 1, 6, 7, 8. Both carry a wave centred on the max's index with opposite signs: $V \approx -66\,(M - 0.89 S^+)\cos(42.75°\,\Delta)$, and $m$ the mirror image (63 per unit gap); they cancel to within 8. $m$ ranks the max first among older inputs (89–99% at max ages 1–8); the runner-up exists only in the sum. The cosine shape is forced by the readout columns; approximate formulas do not reproduce the answers (63% against 97%).
- **Interventions.** Removing the comparators' share of the clock pattern while the max stands (2–7 steps old): the net answers the max in 94–100%. (Comparators set to exactly zero do the same, but an exact zero also imitates a new max.) Comparators are the best of all 1820 four-unit sets. One step before the max is overtaken, removing the wave units' share of the clock pattern: old max answered in 2% instead of 99%; moving only the comparators until $z = 0$: 98.7%.
- **Core.** Thresholds obey $\theta' = B_c[\theta - x]_+ + b_2 h[2] + b_4 h[4] + \varepsilon$ with $B_c\mathbf 1 \approx 0$ (rank 1 of all 1820 four-unit subsets; eigenvalues $0.9375\,e^{\pm i 42.56°}$, 0.043). Unit 2 ($\approx 17.3\max(M_{t-1}, 0)$) re-supplies the level every step ($b_2 \approx 0.05$): the ersatz bias; without it 28% accuracy. Unit 4 feeds $\theta_7, \theta_1$ and not $\theta_6, \theta_8$, so a new max launches the rotation with radius ≈ its excess over $\theta_4$. **The six core units are not a closed system**: cutting the satellites' weights into them gives 51% accuracy. A first, unverified decomposition (e29): the wave units' feedback is their share of every mode, the level first; the level is a loop through all 13 units (unit 2 alone decays by 0.969).
- **Clock.** Gap term has phase 0°, 36°, 79°, 123°, 166°, 209°, 251°, 294°, 338° at max age 1..9; $z \approx w_4[\alpha(a)e^{i\varphi(a)}(M - S^+) + \beta S^+]$ with complex $\beta$, $|\beta|$ = 0.02–0.38, so the phase of $z$ lags by 8–15° at relative gaps of 0.1–0.3. Best single law for the radius: $M - cS^+$, $c$ = 0.87 falling to 0.75. Level coordinate $\approx 0.936\,w_4\max(M_{t-1}, 0)$ (lags one step). The clock pattern is nearly in the kernel of the readout (comparator and wave halves cancel to 5.75%), yet deleting it from $h_9$ changes 7.4% of answers.
- **Unit 5** is a fifth comparator with threshold $\Theta_5 \approx 0.75 S$ (from the runner coordinate), acting one step late; its pulse writes the score of a near-top input. After a new max its write mirrors unit 4's; that pulse is what later lets the overtaken max win (0.967 → 0.020 without it). Unit 9 and the clipping of unit 0 at max age 2 keep the standing max below the runner-up.
- **Wave units.** Units 0, 11, 10 are near zero at max ages 2, 5, 6 (only unit 0 is clipped deeply). Their low values are needed when a new max arrives; the exact zeros are not. Unit 12 is not cut.
- **Errors.** 18% of errors: runner-up not positive (zero is the reference). 75%: two of the top three within $0.05 M$. An exact new-max detector in place of unit 4 lowers accuracy (93.1%); removing false firings of unit 4 gives 95.6%, removing unit-5 written scores of inputs below the runner-up 95.9%, both 96.2%. Runner-up/third confusions concentrate where the max arrives at the last step or the runner-up arrived just before the max (e.g. positions (0, 7, 9): third named in 37–63%). "Errors at separations 7–8 from a single harmonic" was refuted.
- **Hypothesis "off-centre circle with an ersatz bias":** supported, with refinements; see `report.md` section 8.
- **Open** (report section 10): why the thresholds need each mode's wave-unit share to a few percent; what the threshold swing does for the comparisons themselves; the short clock's roles; the position-dependent offsets of the runner-up/third comparison.

## Gotchas

- Workflow subagents cannot write `.md` report files (the harness refuses); have them return text and dump it from the workflow journal (`hide/dump_arm_results.py`, `hide/dump_verify_results.py`, `hide/dump_review_results.py`).
- Long bash heredocs with LaTeX can fail to parse; write documents with the Write tool.
- Only 10% of states are in the exact "resting" gate pattern; statements derived from the resting map are linearisations.
- $R^2$ of activations, and uncentred $R^2$ of quantities that all scale with $|x|$, are lenient: a model with no dynamics reaches 0.82–0.997. Use functional tests (accuracy after an ablation) before calling a subsystem closed.
- Pooled statistics hid the real variables several times (wipe step, age of the old runner-up, step of the new max). Split by configuration before interpreting a trend.
- A forcing test that replaces a value by an earlier, larger one tests "low", not "zero"; test the ReLU itself by letting the unit go negative.
