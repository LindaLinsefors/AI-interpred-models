# Verification bundle V4_core

## Summary

Bundle V4 (core), independent re-computation with my own code (fresh seeds 5001-5025, N(0,1), 200k-800k sequences per claim; python -B, CPU numpy). NUMBERS: all seven claims K0-K6 reproduce -> 7 x CONFIRMED. Tolerance applied: <= 0.0005 absolute for weight-derived numbers (K0-K2, compensation values), <= 0.006 absolute for sampled numbers (two independent 400k-seed runs differ by <= 0.001, so this is far inside the 5% / 0.01 rule). Two definitions had to be resolved: K4 'age of the max at t' = a1(t); K6 uses t <= 8 only (with t <= 9 the phases shift by up to 0.5 deg and b_S/b_M at age 6 becomes -0.808). I read the lead's e21/e23/e24 only afterwards; they use exactly these readings.

INTERPRETATION: partly survives, over-read in four places.
SURVIVES: (a) 'Bc forgets a common shift of its input' is real and special: |Bc·1|/(|Bc|_F·|1|) = 0.0036 against a median of 0.48 for Gaussian 4x4 matrices (analytic P ≈ 5e-9; smallest of 2M draws 0.015), smallest of all 1820 four-unit subsets (next 0.064, median 0.57), and 1 is the null singular vector (|cos| = 0.9996, σ_min = 0.0044). It holds in the real network including all side paths: with all four comparators on, |∂θ_j(t+1)/∂x_t| <= 0.011, and a common shift of the four thresholds leaves 0.008-0.014 of itself after one step, against 1.33 for a difference pattern of equal norm. (b) Unit 2's supply is indispensable (zeroing it: 19-28% accuracy). (c) After a runner-up that arrives later than the max, the radius follows the present gap M − S, not the record's increment (Re γ/α = 0.02-0.14).
OVER-READ: (1) 'Nearly closed subsystem' fails functionally. Cutting the ten other units' input into the six core units in the real network (the core then IS the six-unit simulation, checked to 6e-14) drops accuracy from 95.3% to 50.6%; with the K5 compensation (activation R² >= 0.989) to 49.0%; on easy inputs (S > 0.3, both relative gaps > 0.2) 63% / 61% against 99.84% intact; dose-response is smooth (25% cut: 84.9%, 50%: 66.6%). The K5 R² is uninformative for units 2, 4, 7: a no-dynamics yardstick 'all thresholds = running max' gives 0.979, 0.995, 0.984. (2) The 0.04-0.11 M from the other wave units is not a level: it swings 0.003-0.175 M with the age of the max, is only 23-66% explained by unit 2, and removing it gives 54.5% accuracy; keeping only its common-level part gives 58.0%, keeping only its difference part 83.1%. Wave units 14, 0, 10 carry it (level-only: 49.7%, 63.5%, 69.9%). The literal idealisation 'comparators + one uniform level from unit 2' scores 73.5%. (3) Unit 2 is neither a uniform level nor a pure running max: making b2 uniform (changes of 2-4% in four weights) gives 65.7%, while a common shift of the same norm costs 2 pp; b2 leaks |ℓ·b2|·17.3 = 0.080 M per step into the clock coordinate. For maxima older than 4 steps h_2 ≈ 17.3·[M − κ(M − S⁺)], κ = 0.21-0.40 (age 7: 10.5 M + 6.9 S⁺; median h_2/(17.3 M) from 0.63 at S⁺ = 0 to 1.02 at S⁺ ≈ M). The comparator block also writes a common level itself (column sums 0.74, 1.24, −2.55, 0.54; removing it: 71.8%). (4) Radius: z_c ≈ α_a(M − S⁺) + β_a S⁺ with |β/α| = 0.04-0.24, so at a tie |z_c| is still 0.06-0.13 M. c = −b_S/b_M is 0.90-0.945 at every age when the runner-up is the beaten old max (37% of states) but 0.76-0.81 for a younger runner-up once the max is >= 5 steps old; scale-free R² there is 0.72-0.84 (uncentred 0.96). 47-61% of positive runner-ups arriving at max age 5-8 switch no comparator off, and then the radius keeps the OLD gap (Re γ/α = 0.56-0.89). The per-step factor on z_c in the real network is not constant: pooled 0.943∠41.6°, by age of the max 0.86-1.02 in modulus and 36.8°-47.8° in angle, the other wave units accounting for most of the deviation from Bc's 0.9375∠42.56°.

## Objections tested

- **K0 may be exact only by construction and so prove nothing.** (affects conclusion: no)
  - test: Evaluated both sides at every step t = 0..9 on 200k N(0,1) and 200k Unif(−1,1) sequences (seed 5001); checked the sign of w[COMP]; checked h_j = g_j[θ_j − x_t]_+ separately.
  - result: Max error 4.0e-15 (normal) and 1.6e-15 (uniform). All four comparator input weights are negative, so the identity is exact. It is also tautological: the unnamed 'other ten units' term is whatever is left. It supports nothing about closure.
- **Forgetting a common shift might be generic for a 4x4 block or a consequence of the chosen gauge, not a designed property.** (affects conclusion: no)
  - test: (a) ρ = |B·1|/(|B|_F·|1|) for Bc against 2M Gaussian matrices, 400k random permutations of Bc's entries, 400k random sign patterns (seed 5010). (b) The gauge-free form ρ(Q) = |W[Q,Q]·w[Q]|/(|W[Q,Q]|_F·|w[Q]|) for all 1820 four-unit subsets Q. (c) SVD of Bc. (d) Exact Jacobian ∂θ(t+1)/∂x_t through all 16 units on 400k sequences (seed 5011), by gate pattern. (e) Intervention in the real network (seed 5016): shift the four thresholds at one step by ±0.05 M, once along (1,1,1,1) and once along a difference pattern of the same norm, and follow θ for three steps.
  - result: Special, and it holds on data. ρ(Bc) = 0.0036; Gaussian median 0.478, minimum of 2M draws 0.0152, analytic P(ρ <= 0.0036) ≈ 5e-9; no permutation reaches it (min 0.0039); sign patterns: P = 5e-4. COMP is rank 1 of 1820 subsets (0.0038; next 0.064; median 0.57). 1 is the null right-singular vector (|cos| = 0.9996, σ = 2.074, 0.955, 0.190, 0.0044). Jacobian with all four comparators on and unit 4 off (66.1% of step-states): mean |∂θ_j(t+1)/∂x_t| = 0.007, 0.006, 0.001, 0.011. Intervention, all four on: a common shift leaves 0.008-0.014 of its norm after one step, 0.011-0.021 after two, 0.009-0.017 after three, and changes the answer in 0.3-1.1% of sequences; a difference pattern leaves 1.33, 0.98-1.08, 0.69-0.81 and changes 8-18% of answers. Limit of validity: it needs all four comparators on (P = 0.50, 0.49, 0.53, 0.59, 0.64, 0.70, 0.75, 0.78, 0.80, 0.82 for t = 0..9). With 1-3 on (12.4% of step-states) a common shift persists at 0.49-0.52, 0.36-0.43, 0.26-0.34 and the input does enter: mean |∂θ/∂x_t| = (0.34, 0.13, 0.13, 0.55) with 3 on, (0.68, 0.26, 0.04, 0.83) with 2 on, (1.30, 0.15, 0.08, 1.17) with 1 on. That is how a runner-up is written: a positive new runner-up switches off 1, 2, 3 comparators in 10.6%, 46.7%, 28.5% of cases.
- **'Rotates the DIFFERENCES and forgets the common level' could be true of the input side only; the block might itself produce a common level, so that unit 2 is not the only supplier of it.** (affects conclusion: partly)
  - test: Column sums of Bc and the common-level content of its rotating right eigenvector; with P = I − 11ᵀ/4, replaced Bc by Bc·P (input level removed exactly) and by P·Bc (output level removed) in the real network (seed 5020, 200k); random perturbations of Bc with the norm of Bc − Bc·P as control (seed 5021); from K3, the comparator part of the mean threshold by age.
  - result: 1ᵀBc = (0.743, 1.244, −2.549, 0.539). The rotating right eigenvector has 26% of its squared norm on the common level; |Bc − P·Bc|_F = 1.49 of |Bc|_F = 2.29. In data the comparator block's contribution to the mean of the four thresholds runs −0.31, −0.23, −0.14, −0.01, +0.09, +0.12, +0.08, −0.02 M for max ages 1..8 (for θ_8 alone the K3 mean is −0.26 M, sd 0.41). Accuracy: Bc → Bc·P 92.9% (random perturbations of the same norm 0.0082: 91.4-95.1%, mean 94.3%), Bc → P·Bc 71.8%, intact 95.3%. So the block ignores the common level of its input but writes an oscillating common level that the network needs; the second dying direction (0.72, −0.24, 0.15, −0.63) is passed on once with factor 0.26 and is gone after two steps, so the three difference dimensions collapse to a plane.

## Bugs or issues

- No numerical errors found in K0-K6: every claimed number reproduces within 0.006 (most within 0.0005).
- Claims file, K6: the range of t is not stated. The lead's e24_core_clock.py sets sel[:, 9] = False, i.e. t <= 8; with t <= 9 (θ(10), which the network never uses) phases move by up to 0.5 degrees and b_S/b_M at age 6 is −0.808 instead of −0.794. 'Phase of the median' is the phase of the componentwise median of real and imaginary parts. Both should be written into the claim.
- Claims file, K4: 'age of the max at t' has to be read as a1(t) (window x_0..x_t); with a1(t−1) the list shifts by one entry (1.017, 1.068, 1.050, 0.979, 0.901, 0.854, 0.861, 0.918).
- K0 is an algebraic identity (definition of θ, g_j > 0, and a catch-all remainder). Calling it 'holds exactly on data' is correct but it should not be presented as evidence.
- K5 reports only pooled activation R². For units 2, 4 and 7 a model with no core dynamics (thresholds = running max of earlier inputs) reaches 0.979, 0.995, 0.984, so those three numbers do not test the simulation. The thresholds (R² 0.27-0.79 plain and 0.92-0.95 compensated at the last step), unit 4's events (plain: 27% of its firings at t = 9 are false; compensated: 14% of real firings at t = 9 missed) and the functional test (49-51% accuracy with a closed core) are the informative quantities. e23_core_alone.py already computes gate agreement and firing rates, but they are not in the claims file.
- K5 compensation: slaving the wave units to unit 2 along the level eigenvector adds 0.14-0.27 M to the thresholds, about twice what the real wave units add (0.04-0.11 M); a least-squares stand-in would be W[j,2] += 0.059, 0.068, 0.038, 0.087 for j = 1, 6, 7, 8 and −0.052 for unit 4 (instead of 0.085, 0.100, 0.109, 0.191, −0.128). Even that static stand-in gives only 82% accuracy in the real network.
- K6 reports |ℓ·1| = 0.013 as if the level supply did not excite the clock. The relevant number is |ℓ·b2|·17.3 = 0.080 per unit M per step (the lead's script prints it), because b2 is not uniform; it is 14-25% of the typical radius (median |z_c|/M = 0.32-0.58) and the network depends on it (uniform b2: 65.7% accuracy).
- K6 uses uncentred R² on quantities that all scale with |x|; after dividing by M the centred R² is 0.72-0.84 for ages 5-8 (0.96-0.99 for ages 1-4).
- K3 quotes pooled means over max ages >= 2. The wave part and the comparator part both depend strongly on the age (wave part 0.003-0.175 M; comparator part of the mean threshold −0.31 to +0.12 M), so 'they add 0.04-0.11 M' understates what that term does.
- Wording of the interpretation: 'forgets the common level (and with it the input)' holds only on steps where all four comparators are on (66% of step-states: 50% at t = 0, 82% at t = 9); 21.6% of step-states have none on (the block is wiped, typically at a new max) and 12.4% have one to three on, which is exactly when an input is written into the thresholds.
- In v8_extra.txt section (b) the row 'n_on = 4' reports Re(γ/α) = −2.9: that regression is ill-conditioned because most all-on arrivals are negative newcomers (S⁺ unchanged). Ignore that row; the well-posed version is in v9_followup.txt section (1) and v12_unregistered.txt.
