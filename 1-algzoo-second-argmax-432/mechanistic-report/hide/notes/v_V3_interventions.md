# Verification bundle V3_interventions

## Summary

Both claims reproduce from my own code (fresh seeds 5300-6026; my intervention runner was cross-checked against common.run(x, h0=...) and my I2 selection is identical to one written with running_stats). I1: every cell is within 0.005 of the lead's; the only miss is the control at k = 1, 0.0088 against "≤ 0.008". I2: all 21 probabilities are within 0.003. The direction is as stated: k > 0 moves the answer to earlier positions (the clock phase grows by +42.2 to +43.8° per step of age of the max).

The mechanism survived every test that could have sunk it, but three wordings of the interpretation are over-read.

1. "The comparators hold exactly what hides the maximum."
- It is specific to the comparators: they rank 1 of all 1820 four-unit sets, and no other set reaches P(p_M) ≥ 0.5 at every k. The lasting effect is carried by the comparators' own state at t0+1, not by wave or pulse units.
- The comparator state has two parts. Its clock-mode share is what hides the max: removing only that gives P(p_M) = 0.997, 0.977, 0.968, 0.983, 0.964, 0.943 for k = 2..7, with a monotone dose-response; no other mode's share does it (≤ 0.2).
- The rest is the comparison of the newest input with the max: keeping only the clock share makes the network answer the wipe-step input (0.93-0.99).
- The full wipe gives p_M only at exactly zero for k ≤ 4. With 2% of the comparator activity left, P(p_M) = 0.32, 0.22, 0.10, 0.19 (k = 1..4), because unit 9 must fire at t0+1 to cancel unit 5's score for the wipe-step input.
- At the wipe step itself the network answers the newest input, not the max (k ≤ 3).

2. "The clock coordinate is a pending correction."
- Pending and cashed in at the record are confirmed. Removing the whole clock mode with no record following leaves accuracy at 0.914-0.951 against 0.950-0.968 (k = 2..7); removing it 1-3 steps before a record gives P(p0) = 0.02-0.07; removing it at the record step gives 0.965.
- The carrier is the wave units' share of the clock pattern, not z. Wave-only removal gives P(p0) = 0.022 with |z′|/|z| = 0.92; moving only the comparators so that z′ = 0 exactly gives 0.987.

3. "Its radius sets how much, its phase sets which position."
- On the logits, yes: the bump at p0 is +85.5·(M0−S0) at f = 1 and +45.5 at f = 0.5, and after a rotation its peak sits at p0−k in 74-93% of sequences.
- On the answer, only partly: excluding targets that coincide with q0 (hit rate about 0.95 there) or do not exist, P(answer = p0−k) = 0.36, 0.23, 0.40, 0.37 for k = −1, −2, +1, +2. Rotating all five oscillating modes gives 0.91-0.96.
- For f > 1 the promotion saturates, because the enlarged clock keeps comparators on at the record; 95-100% of those intervened states have negative entries.
- Removing the runner mode (0.9735) also sends 55% of answers to t_r.

## Bugs or issues

- I1 control: the claim says P(answer = p_M) ≤ 0.008; on 1.25M selected sequences the k = 1 value is 0.0088 ± 0.0001. The control set (10, 11, 12, 15) is also among the most favourable 7% of comparator-free four-unit sets; sets that include unit 2 reach 0.27-0.46. Report the range over sets, not one set.
- I1 pooling: "pooled over valid t0" mixes t0 = 9 (the network answers position 9, not the max, for k ≤ 3) with earlier steps. Pooled over t0 ≤ 7 the numbers are 0.9992, 0.9999, 0.9746, 0.9407, 0.9920, 1.0000 for k = 1..6; at t0 = 8 they are 0.795, 0.997, 0.420, 0.705, 0.933, 1.000.

- e18_clock_before_record.py docstring predicts "f = 0 removes the old max's lead (answer -> q0)". The result is t_r in 77.6% and q0 in 16.4%. The claims file reports the numbers correctly; the report text should not say the answer falls back to the old runner-up.
- e18_clock_before_record.py prints a "mean shift of the answer given it is within ±3 of p0". That statistic counts answers equal to t_r (which is p0 + 3 at age 2) and to q0, so it is biased towards positive shifts. It is not in the claims file; do not quote it.
- I2 rotation: the quoted hit rates are unconditional. They include sequences where p0 − k is q0 (17-19%, hit rate about 0.95) and, for k > 0, sequences where p0 − k does not exist (24% for k = +1, 46% for k = +2).
