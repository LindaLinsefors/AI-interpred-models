# Verification bundle V2_scores

## Summary

All six claims S1–S6 reproduce numerically from my own code (fresh seeds 5000–6104, 1–4 million sequences per experiment, batches of 200k). I mark S5 CORRECTED only for scope: its "≥ 0.997 in every cell with gap/M > 0.05" fails for gap/M > 0.8.

The interpretation survives several attacks but needs five qualifications.

**What survived**
- **Phase lock of V to the max.** With shuffled age labels, A(d) collapses to 0.5–8.5 (against −62…+82). The trough of V lies within ±1 position of the max in 93–99.6% of states for a₁ = 2..8. The comparators' complex amplitude turns by a median 40.6° per quiet step.
- **Amplitude ∝ M − cS rather than M.** Centred R² on i ≤ 7 is 0.44–0.49 for (M, x₉), 0.71–0.92 for (M−S, x₉) and 0.81–0.96 for the lead's design. A better overall fit is 66 (M − 0.89 S).
- **Specificity.** Removing any four wave units instead of the comparators gives P(argmax = max) ≤ 0.29.

**What needs changing**
1. **The cosine shape is not evidence.** The readout columns force it: V[0..7] is 97.6% a 42.75° sinusoid for random comparator states.
2. **m carries the mirror image of V's wave.** The same regression on m gives a cosine of amplitude 62.6 with its crest on the max (V: 66.2, trough on the max). In s = m + V the (M−S) coefficient is at most 8 in magnitude. With the max removed, m ranks the runner-up first in 42% of sequences, V in 13% and s in 97.7%.
3. **The cosine description of V is not functionally sufficient.** Replacing V by −60 (M − 0.87 S) cos(ω(i − p₁)) leaves the runner-up first in 61% of sequences (true V: 97.4%). The lead's per-a₁ regression gives 84%, and the exact sinusoidal part of each sequence's true V gives 80%.
4. **"m ranks the max first" has exceptions.** It fails for a max at position 0 at t = 9 (0.30) and is at chance for a₁ = 3, 4 with gaps ≤ 5% (0.51, 0.53). The current input (index 9) is the largest entry of m in 46–99% of states.
5. **At a new max, s_t is more than the shifted m_{t−1}.** For old-top-two gaps ≤ 5% it ranks the old max first in 86% of events, against 65% for shifted m_{t−1}. Wiping the comparators at t−1 costs 1.5–2 points at t ≥ 5. The comparators are all zero in only 84–97% of new-max events.

I opened the lead's e15, e15b and e16 only after all my own numbers existed, and no other verifier's folder.

## Objections tested

- **The cosine shape of V may be forced by the readout columns or by the regression and alignment design, not by rotating thresholds.** (affects conclusion: partly)
  - test: (a) V[0..7] for random comparator states h_c ~ iid Exp(1). (b) The lead's regression and alignment on d with the a₁ labels permuted (seeds 5100–5119, 2.86M rows). (c) Without any regression: P(argmin V = p₁). (d) Longitudinal: phase of the comparators' complex amplitude Z_t between consecutive quiet steps.
  - result: The shape is forced, the phase is not. (a) Random states give a V[0..7] that is 97.6% a 42.75° sinusoid plus offset (real states: 96.9%), so the cosine R² of A_i says nothing about the dynamics. (b) Shuffled ages: A(d) lies between 0.5 and 8.5 for all d (true: −62.4…+81.9); the cosine amplitude of A_i is 3.4–3.9 against 53–78. (c) argmin V over older inputs equals p₁ in 0.967, 0.980, 0.635, 0.832, 0.896, 0.865, 0.909, 0.928, 0.060 of states for a₁ = 1..9. Within ±1 position at t = 9: 0.935, 0.976, 0.948, 0.987, 0.996, 0.996, 0.980, 0.833 for a₁ = 2..9. (d) arg(Z_{t+1}/Z_t) has median 39.9°, 40.0°, 41.6°, 42.0°, 40.5°, 39.9° for t = 3..8 (IQR about 35–49° from t = 5), modulus 1.02–1.10. So "centred on the max and turning about 41° per step" stands; "cosine" is a property of U[:8, COMP].
- **The amplitude might scale with M rather than with M − S, and the constants 60 and 0.87 might hide dependence on the max's age, the runner-up's position or the third value.** (affects conclusion: no)
  - test: Seeds 5100–5119 and 5600–5609. Per-a₁ regressions with regressors (M, x₉), (M−S, x₉) and the lead's (M−S, S, x₉), scored by centred R² pooled over i ≤ 7. Complex amplitude K = −Z·e^{iωp₁} of V[0..7] per sequence: its mean in bins of S/M, regressed per (a₁, a₂) cell, and with max(T, 0) added. Literal model −K₀(M − cS)cos(ω(i − p₁)).
  - result: M − S, not M. R² for a₁ = 2..9: (M, x₉) 0.44–0.49; (M−S, x₉) 0.885, 0.904, 0.921, 0.855, 0.713, 0.721, 0.738, 0.728; lead's design 0.947, 0.945, 0.957, 0.926, 0.836, 0.829, 0.832, 0.814. Overall fit K ≈ 66.8 (M − 0.90 S); by a₁ = 2..9: K₀ = 72.7, 77.9, 75.6, 62.2, 53.0, 59.9, 67.9, 65.3 and c = 0.947, 0.950, 0.941, 0.888, 0.843, 0.849, 0.858, 0.875. Per (a₁, a₂) cell the amplitude is 48–83 and c ranges from 0.71 to 1.01; the trough is offset from the max by −0.43 to +0.20 positions. A separate wave centred on the runner-up is small (−2.2 per unit S, against 66 for the main wave). The third value adds −7.9·T⁺ and raises centred R² from 0.900 to 0.904. Literal model on i ≤ 7 (per-state centred R²): 0.905 with (60, 0.87), 0.910 with the best fit (66.3, 0.890), 0.867 with c = 1, −0.086 with c = 0.
