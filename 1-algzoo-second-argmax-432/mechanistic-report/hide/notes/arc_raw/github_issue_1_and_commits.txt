ISSUE #1: How was example_2nd_argmax() trained? | state open | by jsnider3 (association NONE) | 2026-10-19T19:16:31Z | https://github.com/alignment-research-center/alg-zoo/issues/1

I’m working on the 2nd-argmax accuracy-estimation problem from the blog post (https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/x8BbjZqooS4LFXS8Z/algzoo-uninterpreted-models-with-fewer-than-1-500-parameters), the one with the 432-param model. I trained a thousand networks with the zoo's provided recipe, but the example model looks different from that population and I think it might be the result of different training.

**What I trained.**
1,024 independent 16-neuron, length-10 2nd-argmax RNNs with the defaults of alg_zoo/training.py:

- AdamW, learning rate 0.04/√16, weight decay 0.001;
- batch 1024, 2¹⁴ warmup steps, decay to 0.01×;
- 2³⁰ sequences.

The learning-rate column of the zoo’s training logs (train_2nd_argmax_16_10_1073741824_{0..4}.jsonl) matches my schedule to floating-point precision. The training accuracy of zoo_2nd_argmax(16, 10, seed=0..4) falls inside my population’s spread at every checkpoint from 2²⁴ to 2³⁰ sequences. At the end they sit in the 13th–68th percentiles.

**What looks different.**
I measured the logit margin (the correct logit minus the largest other logit) on 2¹⁶ N(0,1) sequences. The example model’s median |margin| is 8.2:

- my population’s median is 4.35 (95th percentile 6.19), and none of the 1,024 is as large;
- zoo seeds 0–4 are 2.9–5.6;
- it is also larger than every network in my population that uses the same one-step-late write-pointer strategy, including those within a point of its accuracy.

Its weight norms are ordinary, at about the 66th percentile, and its accuracy is about the 75th percentile.

Estimators fitted on my population transfer well to seeds 0–4, to within about 0.5 points, but overestimate the example model by 1.5–2.3 points.

**What I noticed in the bucket.**
alg/one_layer_16_hidden_94_acc_2nd_argmax.pth is dated 2024-11-28, a year before the zoo (2025-12-12). It sits next to 2nd_argmax_32_17179869184.pth, which is 2³⁴ sequences. The bucket has no training log for it, and the README says it isn’t officially part of the zoo.

Questions:

1. How was it trained: optimizer, learning rate and schedule, weight decay, batch size, number of training sequences and input distribution? Was it made from an earlier version of training.py?
2. Was it chosen from several runs, and on what criterion? Was it changed after training in any way, for example by fine-tuning, pruning or rescaling?
3. The filename says 94% accuracy, but I measure 95.30% ± 0.02 on 2²⁰ N(0,1) sequences, with the target as argsort[-2]. How was the 94% measured?
4. Is its training log or config available?

With the recipe I could train a matching population and check whether the difference comes from training or from the network’s mechanism.

And yes, I used Claude to write this.
