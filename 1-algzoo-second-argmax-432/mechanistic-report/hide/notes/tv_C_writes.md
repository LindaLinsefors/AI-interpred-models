# Tape investigation, verifier of arm C_writes

## Summary

I re-derived the investigator's "writes" accounting with my own code (own tape projector built from the eigen-decomposition, agreeing with tape_common.py to 1.6e-15; work done in the 13-unit space, so no eigenvector gauge is shared) on other seeds, and ran nine attacks. Every number I recomputed reproduces, usually to the second digit: the exact identity, the cancelling kinds of write, the shares by causing input, the leave-out tests, the threshold fits, the unit-5 event formula and the failure of the reduced description (L3: 7.05% of answers changed against the reported 7.2%). I found no train/test leak and no deviation from the tape definition; the scripts do what the claims say. One claim is wrong as an interpretation: "the thresholds do not come from the tape" rests on a variance ratio (reproduced), but when the tape's part of the drive is left out, replaced by its mean, or taken from another sequence, 19-25% of answers change through unit 5's pulse, 15-20% through comparators 1 and 8, and 18-40% through the wave clips; only unit 4 and comparators 6 and 7 are independent of the tape (under 2%). So the tape is not a passive ledger whose thresholds are supplied from outside: its own content shapes the next writes. Three further corrections: (1) the pooled shares "runner-up 77%" and "maximum 81%" overstate what holds at a fixed age (52-62%, and 40-81% falling with the age of the maximum); (2) "the maximum's arrival carries most" is a statement about when the write happened, not whose value it holds: by input value, with gates fixed, the runner-up's value carries a covariance share of 0.97 and the maximum's 0.12; (3) ages do not enter only through lambda^age, because the amount written depends on the age of the maximum at the time of the write (one mix for all ages changes 38% of answers through the comparators instead of 3.9%). Controls that the investigator's reading passed: the accounting is specific to the tape (the non-tape modes have a different composition, and the same named description applied to them changes only 2.5% of answers), and the results hold at other steps and for uniform inputs, where the reduced description is somewhat worse (10.6%). The investigator's main conclusion stands: no description in named quantities reproduces the tape functionally. I did not write tape_v2.md: this step returns findings only, and the harness refuses .md files from subagents, so that file is left to the session that collects the results.

## Corrected answer

Definitions. $h_s$ is the state after step $s$, $o$ the 13 non-pulse units, $A = W[o,o]$, and $a_s = w\,x_s + W h_{s-1}$ the pre-activations. The tape part of the state is $P\,h_s[o]$, where $P$ is the projector on the four tape modes (alt, short, h2, h3). A *write* at step $s$ is one term of $q_s$ in the exact update $h_s[o] = A\,h_{s-1}[o] + q_s$, with $q_s = w[o]\,x_s + W[o,\{4,5,9\}]\,h_{s-1}[\{4,5,9\}] + [-a_s[o]]_+$: the input, last step's pulses of units 4, 5, 9, and the *clip* $[-a_s[u]]_+$ of each non-pulse unit $u$. For a comparator $u \in \{1,6,7,8\}$ the clip is $g_u\,[x_s - \theta_u(s)]_+$ with threshold $\theta_u(s) = -(W h_{s-1})[u]/w_u$. $M^+_{s-1}, S^+_{s-1}, T^+_{s-1}$ are the three largest of $x_0..x_{s-1}$, clipped at zero. "Answers changed" is the share of quiet final states (maximum and runner-up at least two steps old, runner-up positive) whose answer changes when the tape part is replaced by the description.

What survives, from the angle of the writes.

1. Accounting (exact, error $10^{-12}$): $P\,h_t[o] = \sum_{s\le t} A^{\,t-s} P\,q_s$. This restates the network. Its content is that the kinds of write cancel: in the tape scores of ages $\ge 2$ the parts from unit 5's pulse, the comparator clips, unit 4's pulse and the wave-unit clips have standard deviations 1.60, 1.18, 1.12 and 0.71 times that of the whole. So the tape is not a blur of the pulses (R² 0.61 down to $-0.47$ per entry), and no single kind can be read as "the content".

2. Two kinds of write are differences of named quantities and pass the functional test. $\theta_u(s) \approx \alpha_u(a)\,M^+_{s-1} + \beta_u(a)\,S^+_{s-1}$, where $a$ is the age of the maximum of the earlier inputs, with error 0.016 to 0.041 running maxima; replacing the real writes by this formula changes 3.9% of answers for the comparators and 0.4% for unit 4. Both ingredients are needed: with the maximum alone 33% change, with one mix for all ages 38%. $\alpha+\beta$ is 0.85 to 1.08; for units 4 and 7 the threshold is a weighted mean ($0.98\,M$ at $a=1$, $0.68\,M + 0.26\,S$ at $a = 5$), for units 1, 6, 8 the weights swing between $-0.34$ and $1.41$ with $a$.

3. Unit 5's pulse, the largest write, has a clear meaning by event but does not pass: after a positive input below the runner-up $h_{j+1}[5] \approx 18.0\,[x_j - \Theta_5]_+$ with $\Theta_5 \approx (0.49\text{–}0.82)\,S + (0.09\text{–}0.31)\,T$; after a new runner-up the slope is 7.8 to 18.6 depending on $a$, after a new maximum 12.1 to 12.9. The formula has R² 0.993 and still changes 5.5% of answers.

4. When the amounts were written: 54% of the variance of the tape scores of ages $\ge 2$ comes from writes made at the arrival of the current maximum, 34% from the arrival of the current runner-up, 9% from an earlier runner-up that was later displaced. At a fixed age the split is less extreme than the pooled one: if the runner-up came after the maximum, its arrival carries 52–62% and the maximum's 14–26%; if it came before, the maximum's arrival carries 80% while the maximum is 2–4 steps old and 40–48% when it is 6–8 steps old. This is about time, not about whose value is held: by input value (gates fixed) the runner-up's value has covariance share 0.97 and the maximum's 0.12, as two large cancelling parts. The writes made when the maximum arrives hold how far it rose above the old maximum, which is now the runner-up.

5. The tape is not a passive ledger. The investigator's statement that the thresholds come from the rest of the state is true only for unit 4 and comparators 6 and 7 (under 2% of answers change when the tape's part of their drive is removed or resampled). For unit 5's pulse 19–25% change, for comparators 1 and 8 15–20%, for the wave-unit clips 18–40%. The content of the tape at step $s-1$ helps set the size of the writes at step $s$.

6. Ages enter in two ways, not one: through $A^{\,t-s}$ (the age of the write) and through $\alpha_u(a), \beta_u(a)$ (the age of the maximum at the moment of the write).

7. No description in named quantities reproduces the tape. The best one (all thresholds from $M^+, S^+, T^+$, the latest input and their three ages, 586 age configurations) gives R² 0.926 of the scores and changes 7.05% of answers at $t=9$ (22% when the runner-up leads the third by less than $0.05\,M$, which is half of all failures), 3.0% and 4.4% at $t = 5, 7$, and 10.6% on uniform inputs. The same description applied to the non-tape modes changes only 2.5%, so what it misses sits in the tape. A tape built from the writes of the current maximum and runner-up alone changes 20% of answers.

Statement from this angle. The tape holds a decaying, rotating sum of clipped differences $[x_s - \alpha_u(a)M^+_{s-1} - \beta_u(a)S^+_{s-1}]_+$ between each arriving input and a mix of the maximum and runner-up at that time, plus unit 5's delayed comparison of each input with about $0.75\,S$. Most of it was written at the arrival of the current maximum and runner-up and, in values, it depends mostly on the runner-up relative to the maximum. This is established as an accounting and, for the comparator and unit-4 writes, functionally. It is not established as a sufficient description: amounts fixed at earlier events (displaced runners-up, old maxima) and the tape's own feedback on unit 5 and the wave units are part of the content, and leaving them to a formula in the current top three changes 7% of answers.
