=== A. First impressions of the summary (section 1) ===
A1. "It never looks back at earlier inputs" is true of every RNN. What is meant: at every step the state already
    holds the answer for the prefix. "It does not know the step number": statement about the recurrence (trivial)
    or about the state (untested)? Unclear.
A2. U, h_t, "mode", "slow mode", B_c, "comparator block", Theta_5 (capital, others lower-case) used before section 2/4.
A3. Dimension count does not close: tape = 10 scores, "three numbers" = 4 real numbers (clock is 2-d), 3 registers.
    10 + 4 + 3 > 16. The summary never says how the 16 units are split (3 pulse units + 13, and the 13 = 1 level
    + 1 runner + 2 clock + 5 tape modes + 2 short clock + 1 transient + 1 echo, which only appears in 4.3).
A4. (2) says the readout "barely sees" the clock; four lines later V = -60 (M - 0.87 S) cos(42.75 (p - p_M)) is the
    readout seeing the clock through the comparators. Reconciliation (two halves cancel, 6%) is only in 4.3.
    Had to read the summary twice and then 4.3 to see that both statements are meant.
A5. "pending correction" / "held in reserve" (sec 8) versus "V pushes the maximum below the runner-up" (in use at
    every step). What is pending is the removal of V, i.e. the promotion of the old maximum.
A6. "Scaling the clock's radius one step before the maximum is overtaken by 0, 0.5, 1, 2 ..." garden path
    ("overtaken by 0, 0.5 ...").
A7. "a new maximum exceeds all four thresholds" - but panel b shows thresholds up to 1.5 on the scale where the
    maximum is 1; section 5.4 says 94-96%.
A8. "the circle is not there for the readout but for the comparisons" - could not work out what "for the
    comparisons" means; it seems to contradict "the clock is a pending correction to the scores".
A9. "by design" / "on purpose" (sec 8) / "is arranged not to see it": teleology for a trained network.
A10. (1) hides the main fact about the tape: the score at index 9 is NOT carried forward. One step later the slot
    is rewritten by unit 5 as 61 (x - Theta_5)_+ over a baseline; inputs below Theta_5 = 0.75 S leave nothing
    (5.1, 3.3 table, panel d). The summary reads as if every input keeps a value-proportional score.
A11. The summary covers a quiet step and a new maximum. It does not say what a new runner-up does, nor what unit
    5's records are. The four-row table of section 5 is the best answer to "how is it updated" and is not in the
    summary.
A12. "How do you know": three interventions only. No sentence on the kinds of evidence, the sample, the input
    distribution (N(0,1), not the training distribution), nor that the linear picture is exact on 10% of steps.
A13. No uncertainties in the summary.
A14. The position of the maximum is stored at least three times (clock phase; unit 5's record after the maximum,
    whose removal drops 0.967 -> 0.020; travelling zero in units 0/12/11/10). Summary names only the clock.
    This also would explain why rotating the clock moves the answer in under half the cases - never said.
A15. "One identity ties (1) and (2)": s = m + V is a definition, not an identity.
A16. (3): "fires by how far" - three equations would be shorter and exact.

=== B. Section 2 ===

    not know t") is not the reason. The exact fact (to verify): h_{-1} = 0 and no bias => leading zeros keep the
    state at 0 => h_t(x_0..x_t) = h_9(0,..,0,x_0..x_t). Then U h_t IS the network output on the zero-padded
